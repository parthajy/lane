Privacy

Your memory should never become someone else's dataset.

A record of everything you do is the single worst thing to hand to a server. So Lane does not have one. There is nothing to breach, because there is nothing to send.

That is also why Rabbit exists. A memory app that has to call a model elsewhere is a memory app that ships your day elsewhere.

It stays on your machine

Reading, writing and answering all happen on your Mac. No account, no sync, no server holding your day.

An encrypted vault

Memories sit in an encrypted store. The key lives in your Keychain, not in a file and not with us.

Text, not pixels

Lane reads what apps already publish for accessibility. No screen recording unless you switch it on.

Delete means deleted

Pause from the menu bar. Exclude an app or a site. Remove one memory, a day, or all of it from disk.

Questions

The ones everybody asks

Does anything leave my Mac?

No. Reading, writing and answering all happen on your own machine. There is no account, no sync and no server holding your memories. The only download is the app's own working files, once, on first launch.

Whose model is this? Do you use ChatGPT or Claude underneath?

No. Rabbit is ours: we built it, we train it, and it ships inside Lane. It writes every memory, ranks your day and answers your questions, on your Mac. There is no OpenAI, Anthropic or Google model behind the curtain, no API key to paste and no usage bill. That is not a policy we promise to keep — it is the architecture. Lane has no way to reach the network, so there is nowhere for your day to go.

Is Lane recording my screen?

Not unless you ask it to. Lane reads the text your apps already publish to assistive technology, the same thing a screen reader sees. Pictures alongside memories are an optional setting you turn on yourself.

Does it capture passwords or keystrokes?

Never. Password fields are excluded and Lane does not read keystrokes at all.

Do I have to write anything into Lane?

No. The only thing worth writing is your why, one paragraph, once — and even that is optional. Everything else is the work you were already doing.

Will it slow my Mac down?

It is built to be cheap enough to forget it is running. Lane idles at close to no CPU and only does real work when what is in front of you changes.

Does it work offline?

Yes — on a plane with the wifi off. Lane never needed the network to answer you in the first place.

Can I pause it or delete things?

Pause from the menu bar at any time. Exclude an app or a website permanently. Delete a single memory, a whole day, or everything you have.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. Windows is next, and early testers get it.
