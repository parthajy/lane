Questions

The ones people ask about money

What does lifetime actually mean?

You pay once and use every version of Lane for Mac that we ship, for as long as we ship it. It is limited to the first 200 people because it is a bet on us by people who are early, and it would not be a sustainable price for everybody.

What happens if you disappear?

Lane keeps working. It needs no server, so there is nothing to switch off. Your memories stay in a store on your disk, and you can export them to markdown whenever you like.

Is there a team or company plan?

Not yet. Lane is a private memory for one person. An organisation version is the reason Rabbit exists, but it is not what we are selling today.

Do you take purchasing power into account?

If $9 is genuinely out of reach where you are, write to us and say so. We would rather you used it.
