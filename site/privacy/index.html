Who we are

Lane is made by Partha Borthakur. In this policy, Lane means both the Mac application and the website at lane.so. Write to pb@lane.so.

If you want this in plain words rather than legal ones, read privacy, explained. Where the two differ, this page is the one that governs.

The application

The Lane application does not collect, transmit or receive any personal data. It has no accounts, no telemetry and no analytics, and it does not send your content to us or to any third party.

Everything the application records about your work is written to an encrypted store on your own device. We have no access to it, and no ability to obtain it.

Two optional features send something, and only when you start them: the feedback form, which opens your own mail application with a message you can read and edit before sending; and the contribution of labels for improving our model, which is off by default and which you review before anything is sent.

The website

When you use lane.so we process a small amount of data:

  • Waitlist. If you submit the form, we store your email address, any name you give, and the page you came from, so that we can write to you about early access. Lawful basis: your consent.
  • Downloads. When the download link is used we record that a download happened, and the source parameter in the link. No IP address and no identifier is stored with it.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback through the website we store what you wrote and, if you give one, your email address.
  • Purchases. If you buy a licence we store your email address, the plan and the amount, so that we can support the purchase and meet our tax obligations.

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Your browser stores one flag locally to remember that you have already joined the waitlist; it never leaves your browser.

Who else is involved

We use two processors, and no others:

  • Supabase hosts the database holding the waitlist, feedback and purchase records.
  • Dodo Payments processes payments and acts as merchant of record. They handle your card details; we never receive them.
  • Netlify serves the website and, like any web server, processes requests in order to answer them.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train any model.

How long we keep it

Waitlist entries are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until the waitlist is closed and everyone on it has been contacted. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax law requires us to keep them. Feedback is kept until it has been acted on.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Because the application holds nothing of yours, any such request concerns only the website data listed above.

Write to pb@lane.so and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the UK or the EU and are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Lane is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so on this page and, where it is significant and we have your address, by email. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
