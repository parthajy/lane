Lane/vs Rewind

Lane vs Rewind

Rewind made the case that your Mac should remember your day, and it was right. The difference is what gets kept, and where the thinking happens.

What it is

What Rewind is built for

Rewind is a Mac app that records your screen and your calls and makes that recording searchable, so you can scrub back to the moment something happened. Its AI features send context to a hosted model.

When Rewind is the better answer

You want literal playback. Sometimes the only thing that settles an argument is watching the screen again, frame by frame, and Rewind does that and Lane does not.
