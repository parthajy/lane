Lane vs Granola

Granola is excellent at the thing it does. The question is whether meetings are the part of your day worth remembering, or just the part that is easiest to record.

What it is

What Granola is built for

Granola sits beside your calls, takes the sparse notes you type, and turns them into a clean write-up afterwards with a hosted model. It is meeting-shaped by design.
