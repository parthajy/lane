How it works

Three steps, and none of them are filing

  1. 1
    Press ⌥⇧Space

    In a mail client, an editor, a form, a terminal. Lane does not care which app has focus.

  2. 2
    Say what you mean

    Lane transcribes on the machine as you speak, so there is no upload and no wait for a server.

  3. 3
    Press again to place it

    The text is inserted where the cursor is, in the app you were already using.

The things that matter once you use it

The speech model ships inside the app. Nothing is streamed anywhere, and there is nothing to switch off.

It is the same engine as meetings

The accuracy you get in a dictated note is the accuracy you get in a transcript.

It works offline

On a train, on a plane, in a basement. The wifi is irrelevant.
