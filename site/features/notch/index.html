How it works

Three steps, and none of them are filing

  1. 1
    It sits where you already look

    Under the menu bar at the top of the screen, and below the camera housing on the MacBooks that have one.

  2. 2
    Hover to open it

    The card unfurls out of the tab: recent memories, today at a glance, your commitments, and quick actions.

  3. 3
    Click to keep it

    Click the tab and the card stays open while you work. Escape or the close button puts it away.

Details

The things that matter once you use it

It says what Lane is doing

One coloured dot: remembering, recording, listening, or paused. You never have to wonder.

Record from it

Start a meeting recording, dictate, or capture what is on screen without opening the app.

It steps aside in full screen

The tab sits exactly where you reach for the menu bar, so it hides itself while a window is full screen.

Switch it off entirely

One item in the menu bar turns the tab off, and it stays off after a restart.

Ask it

What you would ask

  • “What is coming up?”
  • “What do I owe people right now?”
  • “Summarise my day so far.”
  • “Show me recent files.”
